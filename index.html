Add a card yourself

For promos and new cards the databases don’t have yet. Only the name is required, but with the card number filled in, the app will swap in the official picture and details once the database has the card. You can add your own photo afterwards from your binder.

Standard format

Cards with this regulation mark or later are marked Standard legal. Change it when the format rotates. Basic Energy is always legal.

Card info

Fills in missing set codes, numbers, and regulation marks for cards already in your binder and want list. Also matches cards you added yourself once the card database has them. That also happens on its own when you open the app.

Sync between devices

Keep your phone and laptop in sync by saving your binder to a private Gist in your GitHub account. Paste the same GitHub access key on each device.

Backup

Your binder is saved in this browser on this device. Download a backup now and then, and use it to move everything to another phone or computer.
